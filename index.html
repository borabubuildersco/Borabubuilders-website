Our foundation

Nearly four decades of building relationships.

Borabu Builders traces its roots to 1988, when it began in Kericho County as Anyoka Borabu Builders. The company has since grown its capabilities across construction and infrastructure works.

Its current website references experience with organizations including Browns East Africa Plantations, Browns Plantations Kenya, Kenya Tea Development Agencies, Kenya Tea Packers, George Williamson and Government of Kenya departments and agencies.
